Welcome

Royal Marina Suites

Royal Marina Suites was created with a simple vision: to bring together the exclusivity, tranquillity and natural beauty of Lanzarote with the warmth of genuine hospitality.

As a family-owned hotel, we believe that luxury is found not only in beautiful surroundings, but in quality service, attention to detail, cleanliness and personal care. Our aim is to create a peaceful and memorable stay for every guest who chooses us.

Located in the elegant marina of Puerto Calero, our hotel offers a calm retreat where guests can relax, explore the island and enjoy the unique atmosphere that makes Lanzarote so special.

The People Behind Your Stay

Founders & Owners

Susana & Miguel

The vision behind Royal Marina Suites

Royal Marina Suites is the result of the vision and dedication of Susana and Miguel. Originally from Gran Canaria and southern Spain respectively, they have called Lanzarote home for more than three decades.

Their dream was to create a hotel that reflected the elegance, tranquillity and character of the island while preserving the warmth of a family business.

Although English is not their first language, they are always delighted to greet guests, share a smile and, whenever possible, have a conversation about the island they love.
