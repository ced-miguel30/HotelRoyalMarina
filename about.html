# Our Story ## Welcome to Hotel Royal Marina Suites Boutique Hotel Royal Marina Suites Boutique was created with a simple vision: to bring together the exclusivity, tranquillity and natural beauty of Lanzarote with the warmth of genuine hospitality. As a family-owned hotel, we believe that luxury is not only found in beautiful surroundings, but in the quality of service, attention to detail and personal care that every guest receives. Our philosophy is built around comfort, cleanliness, authenticity and creating memorable experiences for those who choose to stay with us. Located in the elegant marina of Puerto Calero, our hotel offers a peaceful retreat where guests can relax, explore the island and enjoy the unique atmosphere that makes Lanzarote so special. Behind every stay is a dedicated team committed to ensuring that your experience is as enjoyable and effortless as possible. --- # Meet Our Team ## Susana & Miguel ### Founders & Owners Hotel Royal Marina Suites Boutique is the result of the vision and dedication of Susana and Miguel. Originally from Gran Canaria and southern Spain respectively, they have called Lanzarote home for more than three decades. Their dream was to create a hotel that reflected the elegance, tranquillity and character of the island while maintaining the warmth of a family business. Although English is not their first language, they are always delighted to greet guests and share their passion for Lanzarote. Their hands-on approach and personal commitment continue to shape the welcoming atmosphere that defines the hotel today. --- ## Carmen Rodríguez ### Department Manager Carmen oversees the day-to-day operations of the hotel, ensuring that every department works together seamlessly to deliver the highest standards of service. Her attention to detail, professionalism and commitment to guest satisfaction help ensure that every stay reflects the quality and care that our guests expect. --- ## Esmeralda Acosta ### Head of Human Resources While guests may not often meet Esmeralda during their stay, her contribution is essential to the success of the hotel. She is responsible for supporting and developing the team behind the scenes, helping create the positive working environment that allows our staff to provide exceptional service every day. --- ## José Pimienta ### Reception Manager José leads our reception team and is often one of the first friendly faces guests encounter upon arrival.
