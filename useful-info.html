Emergencies & health

112

Emergencies: call 112 (free, 24 hours, multilingual).

Hospiten Lanzarote — private hospital with emergency department. Camino Lomo Gordo s/n, Puerto del Carmen. Tel. +34 928 59 61 00.

Pharmacies: there is a parapharmacy in Puerto Calero. The nearest pharmacies are in Puerto del Carmen and Tías.

Reception can help you 24 hours a day: press 9 from your room.

Public bus (guagua)

The Puerto Calero bus stop is at the entrance to Puerto Calero, a short walk from the hotel.

Line 161 connects Puerto Calero with the airport and Playa Blanca.

Puerto del Carmen: line 161 stops along the seafront.

To Arrecife: change at Matagorda to line 2 or 3.

Timetables and fares change seasonally — please check arrecifebus.com before travelling.

Taxi

Reception will order a taxi for you. Just ask, or press 9 from your room.

Parking

Free private parking at the hotel entrance — you can't miss it.

Supermarket

The nearest supermarket is HiperDino Express in the new shopping centre in Puerto Calero.

Luggage storage

You can leave your luggage at reception on your departure day, or if you arrive before check-in.

In your suite

  • Every suite has a safe.
  • Please switch off the air conditioning when you leave the suite.
  • If you have any problem with the TV or any other equipment, please contact reception.

House rules

  • Royal Marina Suites is an adults-only hotel.
  • Pets are not allowed.
  • Smoking is permitted on suite terraces only, never inside the hotel.
  • Please keep noise to a minimum after 23:00.

Laundry

We do not offer a laundry service. Please ask at reception.
